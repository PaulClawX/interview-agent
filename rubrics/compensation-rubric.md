# Offer Package & Compensation Rubric / Offer 薪酬包评分量表

This rubric scores the quality of an offer estimate and negotiation plan for AI roles.

本量表用于评估 AI 岗位 offer package 估算与谈判方案的质量。

## 1) Market Data Alignment / 市场数据对齐

- Excellent: cites multiple current sources (e.g., Levels.fyi, public pay ranges, recent offer reports, H1B/LCA where relevant).
- Qualified: uses 1-2 sources but may be less recent or less specific.
- Weak: based mainly on memory or generic averages.

优秀：多个且较新的公开来源交叉验证（Levels.fyi、公开薪酬区间、近期 offer、必要时 H1B/LCA）。
合格：有来源但数量少或更新度一般。
较弱：主要靠经验猜测或泛泛均值。

## 2) Components Breakdown / 组件拆分

- Base salary: tied to location and level.
- Equity (RSU/Options): grant size, vesting, liquidity assumptions.
- Bonus: sign-on, annual target, relocation when applicable.
- Refreshers: expected ongoing equity where relevant.

Base：结合地点与级别。
Equity：授予规模、归属计划、流动性假设。
Bonus：签字/年度/搬迁等。
Refreshers：持续性股权预期（如适用）。

## 3) Negotiation Levers / 谈判杠杆

- Competing offers from comparable companies/levels.
- Scarce skills premium (AI Infra, LLM serving optimization, evaluation, etc.).
- Team signal: must-hire, urgent headcount, strong interview feedback.
- Constraints: visa, start date, remote policy, risk tolerance.

竞对 offer、稀缺技能溢价、团队强信号，以及明确约束条件。

## 4) Company Stage Adjustments / 公司阶段差异

- Big tech: structured bands, higher liquidity, predictable refreshers.
- Pre-IPO: larger equity upside but liquidity timing risk.
- Early stage: high variance; require scenario analysis and dilution assumptions.

大厂：带宽更结构化、流动性更强。
Pre-IPO：股权潜在更大但流动性有不确定性。
早期：方差极大，需要情景分析与稀释假设。
