# AI/ML Resume Rubric / AI/ML 简历评分量表

This rubric is designed for AI/ML resumes (MLE, Applied Scientist, Research Engineer, AI Infra, LLM/Agent roles).

本量表用于评估 AI/ML 方向简历（算法/工程/研究/Infra/LLM Agent 等岗位）。

## Core Principles / 核心原则

- High information density, low cognitive load, outcome-oriented.
- 高信息密度、低阅读成本、强结果导向。

## Scoring System / 分数解释

| Score | English | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90–100 | Strong hire | 强烈推荐 |
| 80–89 | Interview worthy | 值得面试 |
| 70–79 | Qualified but average | 合格但一般 |
| 60–69 | Significant issues | 问题较多 |
| <60 | Likely filtered out | 大概率被筛 |

## 1) Information Hierarchy (15) / 信息层级 (15)

- Excellent (13–15): clear sections, strong highlights, scan in 5–10s.
- Good (8–12): readable but key strengths not obvious.
- Poor (0–7): wall of text, unclear priority.

优秀：结构清晰、重点突出、5-10 秒能看出亮点。
一般：能读但重点不聚焦。
较差：信息堆叠、主次不分。

## 2) Conciseness & Whitespace (10) / 简洁与留白 (10)

- Excellent (9–10): clean spacing, no redundancy.
- Deductions: overcrowded lines, repeated content, noisy formatting.

优秀：节制、干净、有呼吸感。
扣分：过密、重复、版式噪声大。

## 3) Technical Depth & Mechanism (25) / 技术深度与机制 (25)

- Show how and why, not only what.
- Include model/algorithm choices, architecture decisions, evaluation, failure modes, tradeoffs.

强调“怎么做/为什么这么做”，而不是只写“做了什么”。
要能讲清：模型/算法选择、架构决策、评估方法、失败模式与权衡。

## 4) Measured Impact (25) / 量化影响 (25)

- Prefer numbers: quality metrics, latency, cost, throughput, ROI, adoption, revenue, risk reduction.
- Do not fabricate. Use placeholders if needed.

尽量量化：效果指标、延迟、成本、吞吐、业务指标、采用率、风险降低等。
不要编造数据；缺失用占位符并补充口径。

## 5) AI Domain Signal (15) / 领域信号 (15)

- Demonstrate at least 1-2 strong tracks aligned to the role (LLM/RAG/Agents/Multimodal/Recsys/CV/NLP/RL/Infra/Eval/Safety).

至少有 1-2 个与岗位强相关的方向深度（LLM/RAG/Agent/多模态/推荐/CV/NLP/RL/Infra/评测/安全等）。

## 6) Action-Oriented Writing (10) / 行动导向写作 (10)

- Bullets follow: Action + Mechanism + Scale + Outcome.
- Make personal ownership explicit (avoid vague “we”).

子弹点推荐结构：行动 + 技术机制 + 规模 + 结果。
明确个人贡献，避免泛泛的“我们做了……”。
