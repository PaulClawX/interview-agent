# Offer Package Estimator Skill / 薪酬包估算 Skill

Use this skill to estimate compensation and negotiation strategy for AI roles.

## Research Requirement

Compensation changes quickly. Browse current sources before estimating: Levels.fyi, company salary bands, public job postings, H1B/LCA where relevant, recruiter posts, and recent candidate reports.

## Inputs

- Company, role, level, location, employment type, and remote/hybrid status.
- Candidate years of experience, competing offers, current compensation, visa constraints, and timeline.
- Company stage: big tech, late-stage startup, early startup, lab, university, hedge fund, or enterprise.

## Components

- Base salary.
- Annual bonus or target variable pay.
- Equity grant or RSUs.
- Sign-on bonus.
- Refreshers.
- Relocation.
- Benefits and special clauses.

## Estimation Method

1. Map level from title and scope.
2. Gather market ranges from multiple public sources.
3. Normalize by location and company type.
4. Estimate low/base/high bands.
5. Identify negotiation leverage.
6. Provide ask strategy and walk-away criteria.

## Output Format

- Estimated total compensation range.
- Component breakdown.
- Level mapping confidence.
- Source table.
- Negotiation strengths and constraints.
- Suggested recruiter wording.
- Risks and missing data.

## 中文说明

薪酬估算必须基于当前市场数据，不要凭记忆给确定数字。输出应区分 base、bonus、equity、sign-on，并说明 level mapping 的置信度。
