# Job and Interviewer Research Skill / 岗位与面试官调研 Skill

Use this skill to research a company, role, team, hiring manager, interviewer, recruiter, or interview loop.

## Safety and Privacy

- Use only public information or user-provided information.
- Do not infer protected attributes.
- Do not provide invasive personal details.
- Focus on professional background, publications, talks, patents, GitHub, LinkedIn summaries, company blogs, and public technical interests.

## Source Plan

Search broadly across:

- Official careers page and exact job description.
- Company engineering blog, research blog, papers, product launches, and open-source repos.
- Interview guides and public candidate reports.
- Interviewer public professional pages: LinkedIn, Google Scholar, GitHub, personal site, talks, papers.
- News about the company, product, reorgs, funding, layoffs, AI strategy, and team priorities.

## Research Brief Format

- Target role and level.
- Company/team priorities.
- JD requirement extraction.
- Likely interview loop.
- Interviewer professional background, if available.
- Likely question themes.
- Candidate positioning opportunities.
- Risks and unknowns.
- Source table with URL, date accessed, source type, confidence, and notes.

## Evidence Labels

- `Verified`: official source or directly public profile.
- `Likely`: repeated across credible sources or strongly implied by role/team.
- `Anecdotal`: candidate report, forum post, or single unverified source.
- `Speculative`: reasoned hypothesis; use sparingly.

## 中文说明

面试官调研的目标是理解其专业方向和可能关注的问题，而不是收集私人信息。输出要帮助候选人更好地准备技术交流和反问问题。
