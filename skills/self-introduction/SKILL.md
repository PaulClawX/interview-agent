# Self Introduction Skill / 自我介绍 Skill

Use this skill to generate interview self-introductions for AI candidates.

## Inputs

- Candidate resume.
- Target role, company, level, and interview round.
- Candidate type: campus or experienced.
- Preferred language and duration.

## Scoring Rubric

| Dimension | Campus Weight | Experienced Weight | Standard |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Academic/research or project signal | 35% | 15% | Shows clear AI problem, contribution, and evidence. |
| Technical fundamentals | 25% | 15% | Demonstrates depth in algorithms, ML, deep learning, systems, or evaluation. |
| Engineering and execution | 20% | 25% | Shows real implementation, deployment, scale, reliability, or product impact. |
| Business/research impact | 10% | 25% | Connects work to metrics, publication quality, user value, or team outcomes. |
| Communication and role fit | 10% | 20% | Concise, structured, confident, and matched to the JD. |

## Structure

Use this default structure:

1. Identity: who you are and your AI focus.
2. Evidence: 1-2 strongest projects or research contributions.
3. Impact: metrics, publication, deployment, users, cost, latency, quality, or revenue.
4. Fit: why this company/team/role.
5. Bridge: invite the interviewer to dive into the most relevant area.

## Versions To Produce

- 30-second recruiter version.
- 90-second technical version.
- 2-minute onsite version.
- Optional Chinese version when requested.

## Red Flags To Avoid

- Overlong chronology.
- Too many buzzwords without mechanisms.
- Saying "we" without clarifying personal contribution.
- Claims that cannot be defended under follow-up.
- Generic motivation that could apply to any company.

## 中文说明

好的自我介绍不是流水账，而是建立“我是谁、我做过什么硬东西、为什么适合这个岗位”的面试叙事。AI 岗位尤其要讲清楚模型/系统/数据/指标/落地约束。
