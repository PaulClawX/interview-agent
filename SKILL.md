# Interview Agent Root Skill

Use this skill when the user asks for end-to-end AI interview preparation, including resume improvement, job/interviewer research, self-introduction, mock interview questions, mock interview process, pass probability, or offer package estimation.

## Core Principle

Do deep research before giving role-specific advice. AI interview content changes quickly, and company loops differ by team, level, location, and date.

## Workflow

1. Intake: collect resume, target role, company, level, location, job description, timeline, interview stage, recruiter notes, and known interviewers.
2. Research: search official sources, current job descriptions, engineering blogs, recent interview guides, public interview experiences, GitHub prep repositories, and compensation sources.
3. Resume and positioning: use `skills/resume-rewrite/SKILL.md`.
4. Role and interviewer research: use `skills/job-and-interviewer-research/SKILL.md`.
5. Self introduction: use `skills/self-introduction/SKILL.md`.
6. Mock questions and flow: use `skills/mock-question-generator/SKILL.md` and `skills/mock-interview-flow/SKILL.md`.
7. Pass probability: use `skills/pass-probability/SKILL.md`.
8. Offer package: use `skills/offer-package-estimator/SKILL.md`.

## Required Final Structure For Full Runs

- Executive summary
- Research sources and confidence
- Resume scorecard and edits
- Positioning narrative and self-introduction
- Mock interview plan
- Question bank
- Pass probability range
- Offer package range
- 7-day and 30-day prep plan

## Bilingual Note

Default to English. Provide Chinese explanations when the user asks in Chinese, is preparing for Chinese interviews, or needs bilingual scripts.
