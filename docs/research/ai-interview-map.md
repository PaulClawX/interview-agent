# AI Interview Map

## Common AI Role Families

- Machine Learning Engineer: applied modeling, feature/data pipelines, experimentation, deployment, product metrics.
- Research Engineer: reproducing papers, improving models, running experiments, building training/evaluation infrastructure.
- Applied Scientist: research-to-product translation, statistics, causal reasoning, experimentation, model quality.
- AI Infrastructure Engineer: distributed training, inference serving, GPU efficiency, observability, reliability, cost.
- LLM/Agent Engineer: RAG, tool use, memory, orchestration, evaluation, safety, latency, cost, product integration.
- Research Scientist: novelty, papers, theory, empirical rigor, research taste, publication and collaboration record.

## Common Round Types

- Coding and algorithms.
- ML fundamentals.
- ML system design.
- Research or project deep dive.
- Data and experimentation.
- Behavioral and leadership.
- Team match.

## High-Frequency AI Themes

- Transformer internals and attention tradeoffs.
- Embeddings, retrieval, ranking, and evaluation.
- RAG quality, hallucination mitigation, and citations.
- Agent tool-use reliability and guardrails.
- Model serving latency, batching, quantization, caching, and cost.
- Distributed training and GPU memory constraints.
- Offline metrics vs online metrics and A/B testing.
- Data quality, labeling, drift, privacy, and safety.
