# Deep Research Method

This repository requires web research for company-specific, role-specific, interviewer-specific, compensation, and current-market tasks.

## Source Categories

| Category | Examples | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official | Careers pages, JDs, recruiter guides, engineering blogs, research blogs | Ground truth for role requirements and company priorities |
| Technical | Papers, GitHub repos, architecture posts, conference talks | AI domain and team-specific technical signal |
| Interview experience | LeetCode Discuss, Reddit, Glassdoor-style reports, blogs, GitHub notes | Anecdotal interview themes and question styles |
| Compensation | Levels.fyi, public pay bands, H1B/LCA, recruiter posts, offer reports | Offer package ranges and negotiation anchors |
| Market/news | Funding, product launches, layoffs, reorgs, AI strategy news | Headcount and business context |

## Evidence Quality

- High confidence: official source, exact JD, public profile, repeated current compensation data.
- Medium confidence: multiple independent interview reports or credible market sources.
- Low confidence: single anecdotal post, outdated source, unverifiable forum claim.

## Minimum Research Pass

For a full interview plan, collect at least:

- 1 exact or similar current JD.
- 1 official company technical source.
- 3 recent interview-experience sources when available.
- 2 compensation sources for offer estimation.
- Public interviewer information if interviewer names are provided.

## Answer Discipline

- Cite URLs and access dates.
- Mark anecdotes as anecdotes.
- Do not copy long copyrighted content.
- Do not invent exact salary or probability values.
- Explain assumptions and what evidence would change the estimate.
